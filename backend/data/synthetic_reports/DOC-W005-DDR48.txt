Survey at 3,400 m: inclination 31° (from 27°), dogleg 8°/30 m — above the 5°/30 m programme limit. Unplanned build caused by a formation push at the Kopili bedding. Directional driller advised to hold and drop back to 27°.
=== PAGE 5 | SECTION Stuck Pipe Incident | DEPTH 3390-3430 ===
Mechanical sticking at 3410m due to key seating in dogleg. Torque increased from 12 kN·m to 28 kN·m over 30 minutes. Root cause: key seat formation at 3400m dogleg (8°/30m). Insufficient reaming during trip out. Mitigation: back-reamed to 3350m, reamed down with under-reamer, freed pipe. Modified tripping procedures to include reaming every stand. NPT 24 hrs.
=== PAGE 6 | SECTION Revised Tripping Procedure | DEPTH 3350-3450 ===
Revised procedure for the Kopili interval: ream and back-ream every stand through doglegs >5°/30 m; limit trip speed to 2 min/stand; record pick-up/slack-off weights every stand; stop and circulate if overpull exceeds 50 kN. Lesson: keep doglegs below 5°/30 m in the Kopili.
