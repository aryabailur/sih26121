**Subject:** SIH 2026 · SIH26121 (Nearby Wells Intelligence System) — request for 2–3 anonymised drilling report pages

Dear Sir / Madam,

We are team **[TEAM NAME]** from **[COLLEGE]**, working on problem statement **SIH26121 — Nearby Wells
Intelligence System**. Our prototype reads drilling reports, extracts the events in them (losses, kicks, stuck pipe,
fishing, NPT) with their depth and formation, connects them to the active well's current depth and raises
explainable alerts that cite the report page.

We have validated the pipeline on public records — 1,970 real offshore well histories published by the Norwegian
Offshore Directorate, with 94 % precision on a hand-checked held-out sample. To show the jury that it also works on
Oil India's own document format, we would be grateful for **two or three pages** from any of:

- a Daily Drilling Report (the time-log / remarks page),
- the drilling-summary section of a Well Completion Report,
- a mud-log or NPT report page.

**Anonymise as you see fit** — well names, coordinates, dates and people's names can be blanked or replaced
(e.g. "Well A"). Scanned copies are fine; the prototype reads image-only PDFs with OCR.

**How we will handle them:** used only for the SIH demonstration; kept on our laptop (the prototype runs fully
offline); never published or added to our code repository; deleted after the finale, or earlier on request.

If pages cannot be shared, a blank DDR template or the list of its sections and column headings would still help
us match the format.

Thank you for your time and guidance.
